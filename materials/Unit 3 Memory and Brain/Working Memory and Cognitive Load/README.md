# Memory Quest: Working Memory and Cognitive Load

A voiced, story-driven IB Psychology lesson (Unit 3: Memory, Models and the Brain) for students working alone at their own pace. They test their own working memory, replicate Landry & Bartling (2011) and Sana et al. (2013) informally, and experience intrinsic, extraneous and germane cognitive load.

**Time:** about 110 minutes for the core chapters, about 140 with the optional ones.

## What a teacher needs to know
- **Headphones:** the characters speak every line, and two tasks need sound. Students can mute the voices or the sound from the top bar.
- **Out loud:** two tasks ask students to chant ("one, two" and "the, the, the"). Warn the room.
- **Teacher button:** notes for each chapter, a lesson plan, and presenter mode, which unlocks Continue so you can click through on a projector. Arrow keys or a clicker (Page Up/Down) move between screens.
- **Calm mode:** removes the timer from the three "Static Surge" checks. Students can switch it on themselves; the PDF notes it.
- **Evidence:** the last screen makes a PDF for Elevfeedback. If the download fails, students can open the PDF in a new tab, print to PDF, or copy the evidence as text.
- The tasks are informal classroom demonstrations. They do not diagnose anyone's ability.
