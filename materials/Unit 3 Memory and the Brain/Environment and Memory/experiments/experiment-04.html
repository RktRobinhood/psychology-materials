Study with interruptions

A short experiment comparing comprehension while reading quietly and while responding to unrelated simulated messages.

IB Psychology 2027: Table 3.2 (cognitive load) and Table 4.12 (environmental influences on learning).

Research anchor: Sana, Weston & Cepeda (2013), Computers & Education 62, 24–31, DOI: 10.1016/j.compedu.2012.10.003. This classroom reading task is an original analogue of their lecture experiment, not a replication. No account, telemetry, saved student data or external resources are used.
