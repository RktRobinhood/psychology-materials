Nearby screens and learning

Can a classmate's changing screen affect what you remember, even when you are studying your own material?

IB Psychology 2027: cognitive load (Table 3.2) and environmental influences on learning (Table 4.12).

Study anchor: Sana, Weston and Cepeda (2013), Computers & Education, 62, 24–31. The published peer-distraction experiment measured understanding of a lecture with nearby laptop multitaskers. This offline text reading with a simulated peripheral display is an original classroom analogue, not their procedure. No account, external runtime assets, telemetry or stored student responses.
