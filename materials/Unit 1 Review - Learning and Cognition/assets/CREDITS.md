# Credits

## Art
Characters, environments, props and the narrator medallion were generated with ChatGPT for this lesson by the teacher. Sprites were cut from the character sheets and converted to WebP for the web.

## Voices
Character and narrator voices are synthetic, pre-rendered with Google's Gemini text-to-speech (prebuilt voices) from the lesson's own script. Where a recording is missing, the browser's built-in speech is used.
