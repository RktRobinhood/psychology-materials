What are the three syllabus layers?

Content = psychological knowledge and theories. Context = one of the four applied areas. Concept = a lens for critical analysis. Their roles differ between sections; not every question requires all three.

The animations explain syllabus constraints, not an official IB question-writing algorithm. All example questions are original practice, not IB past-paper questions. Unseen sources are fictional.
