Know what an exam can ask you

The checklist reorganizes the official IB terminology and learning objectives into our six units. Use each heading as a section in your notes. Check each bullet only when you can explain the idea and apply it to an example.

What is examinable?

  • Tables 3.1–3.3: all 27 named biological, cognitive and sociocultural content terms.
  • Tables 4.1–4.12: all areas of study and their learning objectives across four contexts.
  • Table 3.4: research methods, sampling, research considerations and data-related terminology.
  • Six concepts, research practicals, and HL extensions where applicable.

Important distinctions

  • The guide sometimes says “one or more” or offers choices. Learn the category and your chosen case; every option is not compulsory.
  • The guide’s example studies and concept-related lists are not mandatory prescribed studies or exhaustive exam stems.
  • Unseen research studies and scenarios can still appear. Focus on explaining and applying principles, not memorizing answers.
  • Checkboxes are personal readiness notes, stored only in this browser on this device.
