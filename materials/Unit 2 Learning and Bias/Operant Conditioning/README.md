# Escape the Loop: Operant Conditioning

Teacher note. A self-paced escape room on operant conditioning for IB Psychology (Unit 2): reinforcement and punishment, shaping, Thorndike's Law of Effect, the ABC contingency, schedules of reinforcement, Skinner's superstition study and its critics, and a final transfer task on phone habits.

- **Time:** about 70 minutes. Nine locks, four short YouTube videos (each has a direct link if the embed is blocked).
- **How it runs:** locks open in order. Quizzes and matching games need the correct answer; written boxes need a real answer. Questions, options and cards are shuffled per student, and a wrong multiple-choice pick locks that question for 4 seconds.
- **Saved progress:** everything is kept in the browser's localStorage on that device. A reload restores it; **Start over** on the first screen clears it.
- **Evidence:** on the last screen students enter their name and class and download a PDF of their responses to upload to **Elevfeedback**. If the download fails they can open the PDF in a new tab, print to PDF, or copy the answers as text.
- **Verification:** the PDF carries a teacher-verification QR code (the copied text ends with the same details as a base64 code). It summarises browser-side signals such as blocked paste attempts, tab switches and wrong picks. These are prompts for a conversation, not proof of misconduct, and are not shown to students.
