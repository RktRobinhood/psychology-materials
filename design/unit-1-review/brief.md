# Brief: Odyssey, The Long Way Home (Unit 1 Review)

## The idea, in the teacher's words

"A Telltale game based on the Odyssey" that reviews everything for the first test, a full Paper 1 on Learning and Cognition only. Broken into segments that students can play end to end, jump into at any point, or use as review activities. A narrator who alone breaks the fourth wall and introduces the educational content; characters stay in character. Branching pathways that can come back together or lead to multiple endings. Fully voiced with Google text-to-speech, a different voice per character. An option for students who do not want the story: activities with a bit of lesson attached. Gamified: trackable progress, you can lose, drills that vary so it is replayable, a roguelike layer with coins and power-ups (including undoing a choice, which is otherwise final), and quick-time events that are really rapid recall questions.

## Where it sits

IB Psychology (guide for first assessment 2027), Learning and Cognition context, cognitive approach content for Paper 1 sections A and B, and the concepts for section C.

## Learning outcomes

By the end a student can:
- explain each Unit 1 theory with its key terms and one linked example (Section A);
- apply the right theory to an unseen scenario with developed "because" links (Section B);
- use the six concepts precisely, not as slogans, to argue about an area of study (Section C);
- recall the key studies' aims, methods, findings and evaluation;
- recognise their own weakest topics from the mastery tracker and revise them.

## Format

- **Voyage (story):** 8 episodes of 12 to 16 minutes (about 2 hours in total). Each has a cold open, a "Previously on" recap with warm-up recall, experience-first demonstrations, the Chronicler's notes, drills, a big choice or two, timed quick-time recall, and an exam move ("Captain's log").
- **Athena's Library (study):** every topic without the story: voiced lesson, studies, drills, rapid recall, exam prompts.
- **The Oracle's Trials (Paper 1):** 12 Section A, 10 Section B and 8 Section C prompts with checklists, markbands and model answers; three full mock papers.

## Game systems

- Crew as lives (a wrong quick-time answer costs a sailor; losing everyone ends the episode with a retry at 6 crew). Drachmae from right answers, drills and hidden chests (variable ratio, on purpose). Poseidon's wrath from hubris choices shortens timers.
- The Chronicler's stall: 4 random items per visit from 8 (Owl, Beeswax, Laurel, Moly undo, Lantern preview, Lyre crew, Aeolus' bag skip, Amphora double coins).
- Random omens at each episode start; shuffled questions weighted by a Leitner box per question; missed questions return 2 to 4 questions later.
- Six endings (crew, canon, scholar, wrath, doubt, lost), 20 laurels, end-of-episode choice recaps comparing with the student's earlier voyages.
- Calm mode removes every timer. Presenter mode for projector use. Evidence PDF for Elevfeedback.

## Assumed defaults and open questions

- Section C prompts pair concepts with L&C learning objectives (strategies to improve memory, environmental factors, schema, SLT, conditioning); check that this matches how the teacher sets Section C.
- Craik and Tulving percentages and Bransford and Johnson exact means are deliberately not quizzed.
- MSM encoding by store (acoustic STM, semantic LTM) is not stated in the class lessons; consider adding at the Examiner stage.
- Voices: prebuilt Gemini voices with style prompts; a custom Voice Design narrator could replace Sadaltager later.
- Ambience (sea, wind, cave) and music are not included: no CC0 pack is in the repo, and downloading one needs the teacher's approval.
